**Vespertine 1.2.3.** The free, open-source Mac player for surround and hi-res music collections.

If you're new here: Vespertine plays your stereo and surround music from one library (FLAC, DSD, SACD and DVD-Video images, DTS CDs, Dolby TrueHD, DTS-HD Master Audio, Atmos from Dolby Digital Plus), shows every step between the file and your DAC, measures and corrects your room with a USB microphone, and tells you whether your "hi-res" files really are. No account, no telemetry, no subscription.

### New

- **Copy Signal Path.** You can copy the signal path in the inspector as text and paste it into a bug report or a forum post, with no screenshot. Click the copy icon at the right of the Signal Path heading (it turns into a check mark for a moment), or right-click the path and choose Copy Signal Path. The text has the Vespertine and macOS versions, the status badges, each step with what it does and whether it leaves the signal untouched or changes it, and the output's stream: its channels, the ones Vespertine fills, the speaker setup, the number of streams and the buffer size. Atmos rendered by macOS copies too.
- **FiiO KA15.** Vespertine sends DSD to the FiiO KA15 as DoP from the start, with nothing to switch on. The developer tried one KA15 and DSD64 to DSD256 played natively. The DSD rates of the 48 kHz family are untested on it.

### Fixed

- Your DoP choice for a DAC follows the DAC to any USB port. A DAC without a USB serial number, like the FiiO KA15, looked like a new device in every port, so Vespertine forgot the choice you made with the DoP switch or the DoP test when you moved it. The inspector offered the test again and DSD kept converting to PCM. A new choice replaces the older ones for that DAC.

### Install

Download **Vespertine-1.2.3.dmg**, or `brew install --cask szeremeta1/tap/vespertine`. macOS 14.4 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.

Already installed? Vespertine updates itself, or run `brew upgrade --cask vespertine`.
