**Vespertine 1.3.0.** The free, open-source Mac player for surround and hi-res music collections.

If you're new here: Vespertine plays your stereo and surround music from one library (FLAC, DSD, SACD and DVD-Video images, DTS CDs, Dolby TrueHD, DTS-HD Master Audio, Atmos from Dolby Digital Plus), shows every step between the file and your DAC, measures and corrects your room with a USB microphone, and tells you whether your "hi-res" files really are. No account, no telemetry, no subscription.

### New in 1.3

- **Tested on a real AV receiver.** Vespertine's surround playback has now been checked on an Onkyo TX-NR717 over HDMI with 5.1 speakers, with a recording of exactly what went out on each HDMI channel. 5.1 and 7.1 files land on their own speakers from 32 to 192 kHz, 384 and 768 kHz files are resampled to 192 kHz and the signal path says so, 5.1 DSD plays as 176.4 kHz PCM with every channel in place, gapless stays gapless, and Vespertine's volume lowers every channel by the same amount. Dolby Digital, Dolby Digital Plus and DTS (DTS CDs included) show on the receiver's display as what they are.
- **BIT-PERFECT on a receiver.** A 5.1 file placed untouched on a receiver's eight HDMI outputs, with the two unused channels silent, is now badged BIT-PERFECT. No sample changes on the way, so it no longer shows CONVERTED.

### Fixed

- **Surround on an HDMI receiver no longer depends on what the link was left at.** macOS describes an HDMI output's speakers by whatever channel count the link happens to be at, and a receiver can drop to 2 or 6 channels by itself. Vespertine took that as a stereo or 5.1 setup and folded surround music down to it, or put voices in the wrong speakers. An HDMI or DisplayPort output that takes eight channels now always gets its eight-channel layout.
- **Dolby and DTS on a receiver play as Dolby and DTS, not hiss.** After a surround song the HDMI link stayed at eight channels and the bitstream went out inside it, so the receiver played it as noise. Vespertine now switches the link to two channels for Dolby and DTS, and leaves it alone for PCM.

### Since 1.2.0

If you're updating from 1.2.0, the 1.2 updates also brought: Sonos rooms as outputs (beta) and a "Will play" line for every output (1.2.4); Copy Signal Path and DoP choices that follow a DAC to any USB port, plus the FiiO KA15 (1.2.3); choosable, sortable columns, skips, listening statistics that survive rescans, and 24-bit / 48 kHz to an Apple TV 4K (1.2.2); one song instead of two for stereo and surround editions, AirPlay 2 receivers, and Speaker Groups in 3D with Fill the room (1.2.1). ### Install

Download **Vespertine-1.3.0.dmg**, or `brew install --cask szeremeta1/tap/vespertine`. macOS 14.4 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.

Already installed? Vespertine updates itself, or run `brew upgrade --cask vespertine`.
