**Vespertine 1.3.1.** The free, open-source Mac player for surround and hi-res music collections.

If you're new here: Vespertine plays your stereo and surround music from one library (FLAC, DSD, SACD and DVD-Video images, DTS CDs, Dolby TrueHD, DTS-HD Master Audio, Atmos from Dolby Digital Plus), shows every step between the file and your DAC, measures and corrects your room with a USB microphone, and tells you whether your "hi-res" files really are. No account, no telemetry, no subscription.

### Fixed

- **Far-away network shares.** Music on a far-away network share (a server across the ocean, or over a VPN) now starts playing much sooner and buffers less: Vespertine now sizes each read to what the link actually carries. On a share 100 ms away a Dolby Digital Plus track is ready in about 1.3 s instead of 2.8 s.

### Since 1.2.4

If you're updating from 1.2.4, 1.3.0 also brought surround tested on a real AV receiver over HDMI, BIT-PERFECT for 5.1 on a receiver's eight outputs, an eight-channel layout for HDMI receivers whatever the link was left at, and Dolby and DTS that play as Dolby and DTS on a receiver. Full notes: https://github.com/vespertineapp/Vespertine/tree/main/docs/releases

### Install

Download **Vespertine-1.3.1.dmg**, or `brew install --cask szeremeta1/tap/vespertine`. macOS 14.4 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. Signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled.

Already installed? Vespertine updates itself, or run `brew upgrade --cask vespertine`.
